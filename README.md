# cherrybite
CherryBite is a food discovery platform designed to solve the problem of fake reviews and paid food promotions.

Instead of relying on influencers or sponsored content, CherryBite uses community validation where users can review dishes, discover nearby food spots, and confirm or disagree with other reviews.

Our mission is simple:

"Help people discover food that actually tastes good."

Key Features:
• OTP-based authentication
• Location-aware food discovery
• Dish-specific reviews
• Community verification (Tried & Confirmed)
• Trust score system
• Hidden gem discovery
• User profiles and badges
• Follow trusted reviewers
• Real-time notifications
